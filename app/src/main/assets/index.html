Siri & Shortcuts

Control the TV from the iPhone Shortcuts app or Siri, e.g. "Open SmartTube on the TV".

Your key (shown once; copy it now):

  1. In Shortcuts, add Get Contents of URL.
  2. URL: , Method: POST.
  3. Headers: Authorization = Bearer + your key, and X-Remote = 1.
  4. Request Body: Form, with pkg = the app (tap and hold one in Apps to see its name).
  5. Name the shortcut, e.g. "Open SmartTube on the TV", and say it to Siri.

Other actions use /api/key with code: 24 volume up, 25 down, 164 mute, 85 play/pause, 3 Home, 26 on/off (sent as sleep or wake, so the box stays reachable).
